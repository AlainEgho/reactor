name: Plutonium Garden
need: 130
hint: A garden of Plutonium, each sphere on its own post with Lead hanging two cells over it. Snap the Graphite under the Lead from the side and the drop splits the sphere into nine Pellets. Stand two cells off; a fission blast reaches one.
